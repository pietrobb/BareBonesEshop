1. Personal data we process

2. Purposes and legal bases

  • Contract: order fulfilment, shipping, payment, customer account and order communication.
  • Legal obligation: accounting, tax, invoices, complaints and statutory retention of documents.
  • Legitimate interest: shop security, abuse prevention, dispute evidence and internal non-marketing statistics.
  • Consent: analytics and marketing cookies, newsletter or other optional communication.

3. Recipients and processors

We share data only where necessary with payment providers, carriers, email infrastructure, accounting, IT hosting and public authorities where required by law. The current processor list is available in the shop administration.

4. Retention

  • orders and invoices are retained for statutory accounting and tax periods, normally 10 years,
  • transactional email logs are retained for 90 days,
  • marketing consents are retained until withdrawal or expiry of the configured retention period,
  • cookie consents are retained for up to 12 months and then requested again.

5. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection to legitimate interest processing and withdrawal of consent. Erasure may be unavailable before the statutory period expires for data retained under legal obligations.

You can send requests to {{email}} or use your customer account. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority.

6. Invoices and legal duties

Invoices and invoice PDF copies are retained in their original form for the statutory period. Later anonymisation could compromise the validity of accounting documents.
