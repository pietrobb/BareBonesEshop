Accessibility Statement

{{name}} wants this online shop to be usable by everyone, including people with disabilities, in line with the European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882) and the national law implementing it.

1. Standard

We design the shop to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA (EN 301 549): it works with the keyboard and screen readers, without JavaScript for browsing, the cart, checkout, withdrawal and complaints, with sufficient contrast and text that can be enlarged.

2. Conformance status

The shop is partially conformant. Content added by the shop (product photos, descriptions and documents from suppliers) may lack text alternatives; we fix such content when we learn about it.

3. How the service works

You can browse, order, pay, withdraw from the contract and file a complaint online. Each step is described on its page; the order confirmation and the terms are sent to you by e-mail.

4. Feedback and contact

If something in the shop is not accessible to you, write to {{email}}{{phone_sep}}. We reply within 14 days and offer you another way to complete your purchase.

5. Enforcement

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may contact the authority supervising accessibility of services{{accessibility_authority_sep}}.
